# Veyora content production and quality-control SOP

## Before writing
1. Confirm paid order, package, billing choice and approved service terms.
2. Confirm onboarding, industry, service area, approved contact details, offers and logo/footage rights.
3. Identify the intended platform and format. No client credentials through unsecured forms.

## Drafting checks
- Every service claim, statistic, price, guarantee and promotion must be verified with the client or a reliable source.
- Do not fabricate testimonials, reviews, results, before/after stories or customer identities.
- Match package limits and brand voice; use client-approved contact information.
- Check spelling, contrast, captions/alt-text guidance, and mobile cropping.
- Verify license/ownership of photos, videos, fonts and music.
- Flag regulated or safety-sensitive claims for additional client review.

## Approval gates
Draft → Internal QC → Sent for review → Changes requested OR Approved → Final delivery.
Only written, unambiguous approval counts. Client silence is **not** approval. Publishing is a separate permission and is not included by default.

## Revision handling
Log feedback date, requested change, version, editor and remaining revision rounds. Scope expansion needs explicit agreement.

## Final handoff
Deliver only approved assets through an agreed secure channel; include filename conventions, posting captions, platform notes and report when included. Reconcile counts, retain proof of client approval, and record delivery confirmation.

## Incident handling
If inaccurate or unlicensed content is found, stop distribution, notify client, correct draft, and seek renewed approval. If sensitive client data is exposed, restrict access, document the incident, and follow an approved incident-response procedure.
