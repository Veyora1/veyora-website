## Internal approval rules
- Never interpret silence, email opens or link clicks as approval.
- Store written approvals with the client record using an approved access-controlled location.
- Reconfirm approval when material claims, prices or visuals change after approval.
- Never post to social platforms or Google Business Profile without separate explicit authorization.
- Use the fictional review demo only for illustration; it is not an authenticated client portal.
