# Monthly fulfillment schedule — internal working draft

**Start trigger:** verified payment/order and complete onboarding. Do not promise a specific turnaround until service terms are approved.

| Phase | Internal target (illustrative) | Owner | Exit criterion |
|---|---|---|---|
| Order verification | Day 0 | Veyora | Correct product and payment verified |
| Intake and assets | Days 1–3 | Client + Veyora | Business details, rights and factual claims checked |
| Content planning | Days 3–5 | Veyora | Calendar mapped to package scope |
| First draft | Days 5–10 | Veyora | Posts and Google drafts prepared; video edits only if included |
| Customer review | Days 10–14 | Client | Written approval or consolidated change request |
| Revisions | Days 14–18 | Veyora | Within contracted revision limit |
| Delivery and reporting | Days 18–22 | Veyora | Final approved files delivered; report only Growth/Authority |
| Next-cycle planning | Days 23–30 | Both | Approved priorities and assets for next cycle |

Targets are proposed workflow milestones, **not contractual delivery promises**. Delays in client assets or approvals can shift dates. No publication without written client approval.

## Package quantity checks
| Package | Social drafts | Google drafts | Client footage edits | Revision rounds | Report | Strategy meeting |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|---|
| Visibility | 8 | 4 | 0 | 1 | No | No |
| Growth | 12 | 4 | 2 | 2 | Yes | No |
| Authority | 16 | 4 | 4 | 2 | Yes | Yes |

## Weekly review
Each Monday: inspect tracker, check blocked client approvals, verify upcoming due dates, and send only approved customer communications. Each Friday: reconcile delivered items against contracted quantities and update the tracker. Never mark content approved based on silence.
