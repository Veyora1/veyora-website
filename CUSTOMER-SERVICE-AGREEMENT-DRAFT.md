# Veyora — Customer service agreement draft
**Internal review only. Not legal advice. Not approved for checkout or publication.**

## Parties and scope
Veyora Local Growth Co. ("Veyora") prepares marketing content for the purchasing business ("Client"). The exact monthly deliverables are those stated in the selected package at checkout and in the approved order confirmation. Services do not include paid advertising, guaranteed leads, SEO ranking guarantees, customer-message management, or posting to client accounts unless separately agreed in writing.

## Client responsibilities
Client provides accurate company details, authorized logos, photos, video footage, contact information, and offers; warrants it has rights to materials; and reviews factual claims and content before use. Client must not share passwords or payment-card data through intake forms.

## Delivery and revisions
Deliverables, review cadence, and start date are confirmed in the onboarding email. The package's revision-round limit applies to reasonable edits within the original brief; material scope changes require a separate quote and approval. Client must approve content before Veyora publishes it, if publication is separately contracted.

## Proposed billing choices — NOT ACTIVE
**One-time:** One month at the package list price; no automatic renewal.
**Flexible monthly:** Package list price charged monthly, with recurring payments until canceled under disclosed rules.
**Three-month commitment:** 15% off package list price, billed once per month for the first three billing cycles. Total initial charges are Visibility $634.95, Growth $1,144.95, or Authority $1,782.45, excluding applicable taxes/fees.

### Open issues that MUST be decided before selling
- Does the discounted plan automatically end after month three or renew? If renewed, at what price and under what consent/notice rules?
- Can the subscription provider technically enforce three required payments and the corresponding cancellation restrictions?
- What are cancellation cutoff, refunds for work not started, nonpayment handling, tax treatment, and charge-dispute process?
- What is the delivery timeline, service start trigger, and response time?
- What usage rights and intellectual-property transfer terms apply to final paid deliverables and underlying editable/source files?
- What state-law and consumer-subscription disclosures are required?

Do not make a customer accept this draft or display it as finalized terms.

## Results and responsibility
Marketing performance depends on many factors outside Veyora's control. No guaranteed rankings, leads, sales or return on investment. Any metrics reported must come from verifiable customer-approved sources.
