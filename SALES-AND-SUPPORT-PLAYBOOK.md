# Veyora — Sales and customer support playbook

## Target customer
US home-service businesses that need consistent, professional content but lack time or an in-house marketing team: HVAC, plumbing, pest control, roofing, landscaping, cleaning. Start with businesses that have active service operations and outdated or inconsistent social profiles. Do not imply an existing client relationship.

## Offer positioning
- Visibility ($249 one-time/monthly list price): foundational content consistency.
- Growth ($449): additional posts, two edits of client-provided videos, and a report.
- Authority ($699): most content, four client-supplied video edits, report and strategy meeting.
- Proposed discounted three-month monthly-billed rates: $211.65 / $381.65 / $594.15. Not yet available to purchase; minimum commitment requires technical and legal validation.
- Never promise specific leads, sales, Google rankings or return on investment.

## Discovery questions
1. Which services and geographic areas are most important to promote?
2. Which social channels and Google Business Profile do you use?
3. How often are you currently posting?
4. Do you have photos or video footage you own and can authorize for editing?
5. Who will approve posts and how quickly?
6. What would a successful month of content look like?

## FAQ replies
**Do you guarantee leads?** No. We provide content deliverables and can discuss marketing goals, but cannot guarantee results.
**Do you post for us?** The current packages are for preparing content. Any posting access or publishing arrangement must be agreed separately and always requires approval.
**Can you edit our videos?** Growth includes two and Authority four edits of footage you supply and have rights to use. Visibility does not include video edits.
**Can we try one month?** The proposed one-time option covers one month with no renewal; checkout is not active yet.
**Is there a discount?** We plan a 15% discount for a three-month commitment billed monthly. Availability and cancellation terms are not finalized.
**How do we share assets?** After order verification, we will provide an approved secure channel; never email passwords.

## Lead-handling rules
- Avoid scraping private contact information or using unverified business claims.
- Keep a record of opt-outs and comply with marketing and data protection laws.
- Never process payments manually by asking for card details.
- Do not promise deliverables outside the selected package.
- Confirm subscription terms and customer approval before any sale.
