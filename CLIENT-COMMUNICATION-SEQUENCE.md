# Client communication sequence — draft templates

These are manually sendable templates. **No automation is currently connected.** Do not send until order status and terms are verified.

## 1. Inquiry acknowledgement
Subject: Thanks for contacting Veyora
Hi [NAME], thanks for reaching out about [BUSINESS]. We can help you compare our content packages. What service area and marketing goal matter most right now? We can share package details before you decide. — Veyora

## 2. Order confirmation and intake invitation
Subject: Veyora — next steps for [BUSINESS]
Hi [NAME], we've confirmed your [PACKAGE] order for [SERVICE PERIOD] under the terms you approved. Please complete our onboarding instructions and share authorized logos/photos/footage through [APPROVED SECURE CHANNEL]. Never send account passwords or card details. We'll confirm the production timeline once the brief and assets are complete. — Veyora

## 3. Missing assets reminder
Subject: A few items needed for your Veyora content
Hi [NAME], we're waiting on [ITEMS]. We cannot finalize content requiring these details until you provide or approve them. Please reply with your expected date. — Veyora

## 4. Draft review
Subject: [MONTH] drafts ready for your review
Hi [NAME], please review [ASSET LIST] for accuracy, branding, usage rights and offers. Reply with written approval or one consolidated change list. No content will be published without your permission. — Veyora

## 5. Revision completed
Subject: Updated Veyora drafts for [BUSINESS]
Hi [NAME], we've applied your requested changes to [VERSIONS]. Please confirm that the revised claims, visuals and contact details are correct. — Veyora

## 6. Delivery
Subject: Your approved [MONTH] content is ready
Hi [NAME], your approved deliverables are available through [APPROVED DELIVERY METHOD]. Included: [COUNTS]. If you spot a problem, reply with the relevant filename. — Veyora

## 7. Next-cycle planning
Subject: Planning next month's Veyora content
Hi [NAME], what services, seasonal topics or verified promotions should we prioritize next month? Please send approved updates and any new assets by [AGREED DEADLINE]. — Veyora

## 8. Support escalation
Subject: We received your Veyora support request
Hi [NAME], thanks for flagging [ISSUE]. We'll review the relevant asset and respond based on our agreed support terms. Please avoid sending sensitive login or payment information. — Veyora
