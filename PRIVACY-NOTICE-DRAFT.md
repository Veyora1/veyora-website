# Veyora — Privacy notice draft
**Internal draft. Must be reviewed and approved before publication.** This notice must reflect actual systems in use, not intended future features.

## Current static preview pages
The development website includes interactive package selection, onboarding and content review demonstrations. Those demonstrations process information in the visitor's browser and do not send the entered form data to Veyora servers. Copy and email actions require visitor interaction; using an email link may transmit information through the visitor's chosen email provider.

## Information Veyora may collect once operations launch
When a customer contacts Veyora or purchases services, Veyora may need business contact details, company information, service area, brand assets, content preferences, order and billing details, and customer communications. Shopify and its payment partners may process checkout and subscription information. Do not claim Veyora directly stores card numbers.

## Purpose and retention — TO BE CONFIRMED
Information would be used to answer inquiries, provide contracted services, support billing and fulfill legal obligations. Actual retention periods, processors, data-sharing practices, analytics/cookies, access controls and deletion procedures must be documented before this notice is published.

## Customer rights and contact
For privacy questions, contact hello.veyora1@gmail.com. Applicable access, correction and deletion rights depend on the customer's location and governing law.

## Before publishing
- Verify actual Shopify checkout, subscription app, hosting and email provider privacy practices.
- Verify whether analytics, tracking pixels, cookies, third-party embeds or file-upload services are enabled.
- Decide retention schedule, data-access rules, security and incident-response processes.
- Review jurisdiction-specific legal requirements and ensure this notice matches the real deployed website.
- Do not publish this draft as a complete legal privacy policy without review.
