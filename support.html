Frequently asked questions

What does Veyora deliver?

Monthly social media post drafts, Google Business Profile post drafts and package-specific extras. Growth and Authority include edits of client-supplied footage and monthly reports; Authority also includes a strategy meeting. Compare packages.

Does Veyora post content for my business?

Standard packages currently cover preparing content for client review. Publishing requires a separate agreement and explicit approval.

How do I get started?

Explore a package, prepare an inquiry and email it to Veyora. Once orders are operational, customers will receive instructions to complete onboarding and share approved brand assets securely.

How are revisions handled?

Visibility includes one revision round; Growth and Authority include two. Final scope, delivery dates and revision procedures will be included in the approved service terms.

Can I cancel a monthly subscription?

Subscriptions are not available for purchase yet. Cancellation deadlines, any initial three-month commitment and renewal behavior must be finalized before activation.

Can you guarantee leads or sales?

No. Veyora provides defined content services, not guaranteed leads, rankings, sales or return on investment.

How do I report an issue with content?

Email hello.veyora1@gmail.com with your business name, the draft filename or description, and the specific correction requested. Do not include private login credentials.
