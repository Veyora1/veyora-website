## Four-week organic content campaign
Week 1: Introduce Veyora and explain deliverables.
Week 2: Show fictional/sample posts clearly labeled as examples.
Week 3: Explain review and approval process; answer FAQs.
Week 4: Compare packages and invite inquiries once checkout or manual contracting is approved.

## Lead qualification
Record business name, publicly available business contact channel, industry, region, observed content opportunity, contact date, consent/opt-out status, reply and next step. Avoid collecting unnecessary personal data.

## Sales objections
- “Can you guarantee leads?” No; content deliverables only.
- “Can you post for us?” Not in standard packages; any publishing needs separate agreement and approval.
- “Can we test one month?” Proposed one-time option is one month without renewal.
- “How does the discount work?” Proposed 15% off for three monthly payments, pending approved subscription terms and technical enforcement.
