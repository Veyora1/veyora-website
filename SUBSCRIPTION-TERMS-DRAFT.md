# Veyora — proposed purchase and subscription terms (NOT approved or published)
## Purchase options
- One-time: one month of the selected service package, charged once, no auto-renewal.
- Flexible monthly: selected package price charged monthly until canceled under clearly disclosed cancellation timing.
- 3-month commitment: 15% off the standard monthly package price for an initial three monthly billing cycles; not a prepaid three-month charge.

| Package | Standard monthly | Three-month monthly billing (15% off) | Initial 3-month total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Visibility | $249.00 | $211.65 | $634.95 |
| Growth | $449.00 | $381.65 | $1,144.95 |
| Authority | $699.00 | $594.15 | $1,782.45 |

## Decisions required before selling
- What happens after the third payment: continue at discounted price, renew at standard price with advance notice, or end automatically? Do not assume.
- Can the subscription app actually enforce three required billing cycles and prevent early cancellation? If not, do not advertise an enforceable minimum.
- Define exact cancellation cutoff, refunds, charge failures, service start, deliverable turnaround, scope, revisions, usage rights, disputes, and required consumer disclosures.
- Verify checkout explicitly presents total minimum commitment, monthly amount, renewal and cancellation terms before purchase.
- Confirm payment gateway compatibility and test all three options in a non-live setting.
- Obtain merchant/legal review for enforceability and consumer law compliance.

No subscription terms or minimum commitment are currently active merely because this draft exists.
