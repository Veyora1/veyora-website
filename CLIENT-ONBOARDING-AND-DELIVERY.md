# Veyora — Client onboarding and delivery SOP (draft)
## After a verified paid order
1. Verify payment and selected package in Shopify Admin; do not trust a customer-supplied order number alone.
2. Email the customer from hello.veyora1@gmail.com with a secure onboarding request. Do not request passwords. Ask for business name, website, industry, service area, contact person, brand colors, logo, existing social profiles, marketing goal, target audience, preferred tone, current offers, and any photos/videos they own and authorize Veyora to edit.
3. Confirm start date, agreed delivery schedule, revision limits, ownership and usage permissions, and customer approval procedure in writing before production.
4. Build content calendar, draft captions and graphics, edit only supplied video assets within plan allowance, and send drafts for customer review.
5. Track revisions, obtain explicit approval before any publication, deliver final assets and report where included, and record completion.
6. For recurring customers, check active subscription status each billing cycle before commencing the next cycle. For cancellations, apply the disclosed subscription terms and verify status in Shopify.

## Quality control
- Check business names, location, claims, spelling, accessibility, and visual consistency.
- Never invent reviews, before-and-after outcomes, discounts, licenses, or guarantees.
- Do not publish without client approval; no guaranteed leads or sales.
- Client must have rights to source images/video; use licensed assets only.
- Do not store passwords or payment-card details in the static website.

## Example onboarding email
Subject: Welcome to Veyora — let's get your first month started

Thanks for choosing Veyora Local Growth Co. We’re excited to help with your marketing content. To begin, please reply with your business name and website, service area, logo/brand colors, social profile links, target customers, top services, and the offers you want featured. For any video edits, please share only footage you own or have permission to use. We'll confirm your delivery schedule and approval process before producing your first content batch.

## Not yet automated
Payment-triggered email, secure file intake, customer portal, subscriptions and fulfillment tracking require verified integrations and account permissions. This document is an operating procedure, not an active automation.
