## Example — HVAC (illustrative, not a real customer)
**Graphic headline:** Is your AC ready for the next heat wave?
**Caption:** A little preparation can help you spot potential issues before peak summer temperatures. Check your filter, keep outdoor units clear of debris, and schedule service with a qualified professional if your system isn't cooling as expected. Contact [BUSINESS NAME] at [VERIFIED CONTACT] to ask about available appointments.
**Visual:** Clean, branded graphic featuring an air-conditioning unit and three simple maintenance tips.
**Fact check:** Verify service area, availability, any specific claims and the client's approved contact details.
